# Security and Compliance

This project is synthetic and not production-ready for healthcare use.

Production requirements would include:

- HIPAA risk assessment.
- Business associate agreements.
- Authentication and authorization.
- Audit logging.
- PHI redaction.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Secure key management.
- Human review for claim decisions.
- Legal and compliance approval.

Do not add real PHI, real member IDs, real payer contracts, or real clinical records.
